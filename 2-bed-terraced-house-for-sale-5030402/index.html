Description

Mc Peake Auctioneers are thrilled to bring 20 Brindley Park Road to the market.

This is a fabulous 2 bed mid terrace spanning a generous 71 Sqm, Located in a small horseshoe cul del sac.

Ideally located within walking distance to numerous bus stops and Ashbourne town centre, local school campus, GAA grounds, this property is bright and airy with well-appointed accommodation.

The kitchen/ diner is situated to the rear of the property, it comes fully fitted with all appliances including a brand new Fridge freezer, there’s is also a utility off providing loads of extra storage. The rear garden is west facing and is sun drenched, and is bursting with stunning flowers shrubs and has a spacious paved area, providing an ideal place to relax.

On the first floor there are two great sized bedrooms with the main bedroom ensuite, and a separate main bathroom.

20 Brindley park Road would be a perfect starter home and will also be ideal for someone downsizing.
